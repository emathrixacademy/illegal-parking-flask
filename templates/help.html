Step-by-Step: How to Use the Calendar

Navigate to the right month — Use the arrows to go to previous or next month. Click Today to jump back to the current date.
Look at the colors — Scan the calendar for red (high-incident) and yellow (some incidents) dates. Green dates are clear.
Click a date — The right panel updates to show the selected date, total incident count, which cameras were involved, and a scrollable list of every incident.
Browse incidents — Each incident card shows the camera name, time, vehicle type, tracker ID, confidence score, and plate number (if detected). Scroll down to see all incidents for that day.
View evidence — Each incident has a captured image (screenshot from the camera) and a video clip (60-second recording) that you can view directly in the panel.
Search by plate — Use the Plate Number Search box in the side panel to find all incidents involving a specific vehicle (e.g., type UAP 430 or ABC-1234).

Plate Number Search

The plate search feature is located in the right-side panel. It lets you track a specific vehicle across all dates.

How plate search works
Type the plate number in the search box (e.g., ABC-1234 or just ABC for partial match)
Click — results show all matching incidents with dates, times, cameras, and images
Results display below the search box with clickable incident cards showing evidence
Tip: Philippine plate format is typically 2-3 letters followed by 3-4 numbers (e.g., ABC 1234, UAP 430). You can search with or without dashes.

Understanding the Incident Card

Each incident in the list shows these details:

What each part of an incident card means
Camera Name Which camera captured this incident (e.g., Zavalla East Road) Time When the violation was detected (Philippine Standard Time) Vehicle Type Type of vehicle detected (Car, Truck, Motorcycle, Tricycle, etc.) Tracker ID Unique number the AI assigned to track this specific vehicle Confidence How sure the AI is that this is a real violation (higher % = more certain) Plate Number License plate detected by OCR (if readable from the camera angle) Image Screenshot from the camera showing the violation Video 60-second video clip of the incident (click play to watch)
