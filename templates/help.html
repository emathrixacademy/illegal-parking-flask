Welcome to DECONGESTILAGUNA

This system monitors roads in Brgy. Kanluran, Taguig using AI-powered CCTV cameras to detect illegal parking and road-blocking violations. Below is a complete visual guide to help you navigate every feature.

Don't worry if this seems like a lot — you don't need to learn everything at once! Start with the basics (Dashboard and Calendar), and come back here anytime you need help with other features. This guide will walk you through everything step by step.

Tip: Look for the ? icons throughout the system. Click them to get quick explanations of each feature. Turn them on/off using the button in the bottom-right corner.

Step-by-Step: How to Use the Calendar

Navigate to the right month — Use the arrows to go to previous or next month. Click Today to jump back to the current date.
Look at the colors — Scan the calendar for red (many violations) and yellow (some violations) dates. Green dates are clear.
Click a date — The right panel updates to show the selected date, total violation count, which cameras were involved, and a scrollable list of every violation.
Browse violations — Each violation card shows the camera name, time, vehicle type, tracking number, accuracy score, and plate number (if detected). Scroll down to see all violations for that day.
View evidence — Each violation has a captured image (screenshot from the camera) and a video clip (60-second recording) that you can view directly in the panel.
Search by plate — Use the Plate Number Search box in the side panel to find all violations involving a specific vehicle (e.g., type UAP 430 or ABC-1234).

1. General Settings

These two values control when the AI flags a parked vehicle as a violation and how often it re-captures the same vehicle.

Violation Time Threshold — How many seconds a vehicle must stay parked in the detection zone before the AI records it as a violation. Example: if set to 300 (5 minutes), a car parked for only 3 minutes will NOT be flagged. Increase this to reduce wrong detections; decrease it to catch shorter violations. Repeat Capture Interval — After a vehicle is recorded as a violation, the system waits this many seconds before capturing it again. Prevents duplicate entries. Example: at 300 seconds, the same car won't be logged twice within 5 minutes.
Change the number in either field (in seconds)

Troubleshooting

If something is not working as expected, check these common problems and solutions before contacting your administrator.

The page keeps loading and nothing appears
Try refreshing the page by pressing F5 on your keyboard, or click the refresh button in your browser. If it is still stuck, wait about 30 seconds and try again. The system may be restarting.
Camera feeds show a black screen or "Connecting..."
The camera computer (Raspberry Pi) may be offline or restarting. Check the Health Status Bar on the Dashboard. If it shows "Critical" or "Warning," contact your system administrator.
I saved settings but nothing changed
A green "Saved!" message should appear after saving. If you see a red error message instead, check your inputs and try again. Make sure all required fields are filled in correctly.
I am not receiving email alerts
First, check your spam or junk folder — alerts sometimes end up there. Make sure the email address is entered correctly in Settings. If emails still do not arrive, ask your administrator to verify the special Gmail password is set up correctly.
Calendar shows no violations but I know there were some
Make sure you are looking at the correct month. Use the and arrow buttons at the top of the calendar to navigate between months. You can also click the Today button to jump back to the current month.
The system shows the wrong time
All times in the system are shown in Philippine Standard Time (PHT). This cannot be changed. If you are in a different time zone, the times will look different from your local clock, but they are correct for the Philippines.
If none of the solutions above solve your problem, contact your system administrator using the email address below.
